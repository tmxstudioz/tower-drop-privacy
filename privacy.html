1 Overview & Privacy Commitment

Tower Drop: Lava Escape is an action arcade game developed and published by Temoor Ahmed (“we”, “us”, or “our”) under the studio label TEMOOR. This service is provided as an ad-supported game intended for use as is.

Our Core Privacy Commitment: We do not collect, harvest, transmit, store, or sell personally identifiable information (PII). We do not require or offer user account registration, sign-in, profile creation, or social network logins. You can play all 10 world levels completely anonymously.

We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, contacts, photos, audio files, physical addresses, or precise GPS coordinates.

2 Local Device Storage (Offline Game State)

To preserve your gameplay achievements across gaming sessions, the app stores non-sensitive game state locally on your device via standard HTML5 localStorage.

Locally Stored Data Keys:

  • tower_drop_unlocked_level: Integer representing your highest unlocked world (from Level 1 up to Level 10).
  • tower_drop_highscore: Integer representing your highest overall game run score.
  • tower_drop_level_[id]_score: Best score achieved for each completed level (Levels 1–10).
  • tower_drop_total_coins: Cumulative lifetime count of collected rune coins.
  • tower_drop_sound_muted: Audio playback state (sound effects toggle).
  • Gameplay Settings: Ambient music toggle, tactile haptic vibration toggle, and touch swipe sensitivity level.
Local Data Isolation: This information resides solely on your physical device. It is never uploaded to any remote server or third-party database. If you clear the application data in Android Settings or uninstall the app, this locally stored game state is permanently erased.

3 Advertising Integration (Google AdMob)

To provide Tower Drop: Lava Escape as a free-to-play mobile experience, the application incorporates the official Google Mobile Ads SDK (Google AdMob) provided by Google LLC.

The game implements three specific, non-intrusive ad formats designed to respect uninterrupted gameplay:

  • Banner Ads: Displayed only on menus and non-gameplay overlay screens (Main Menu, Level Selection, Pause Modal, and Settings). Banner ads are strictly hidden during active gameplay so they never obscure the circular tower platforms, falling ball, or rising lava.
  • Interstitial Full-Screen Ads: Displayed only during natural pauses between completed levels (levels 1 through 9). Interstitials are strictly prohibited during active gameplay and are permanently suppressed after the final Level 10 Grand Finale victory.
  • Rewarded Video Ads: Optional, player-initiated ads (for example, choosing to watch a video on the Game Over screen to claim +100 bonus rune coins). Bonus coins are credited strictly after verified completion callback (RewardAdPluginEvents.Rewarded) confirmed by the native Google AdMob SDK. Closing the video early or encountering network failures grants zero coins.

4 Google UMP Consent & Player Privacy Choices

The game implements the official Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK to comply with the European Economic Area (EEA) General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and United Kingdom privacy requirements.

How Consent & Privacy Choices Function:

  • Initial Consent Check: When required for users in the EEA/UK, the official Google consent dialog is presented prior to requesting advertising inventory, enabling users to choose between personalized ads, non-personalized ads, or to adjust vendor preferences.
  • In-Game Consent Adjustment: You can review or withdraw your advertising consent preferences at any time directly in the game: navigate to Pause / Main Menu → Settings → “Advertising Privacy Options” (Google UMP).
  • Immediate Effect Upon Withdrawal: If consent is withdrawn or declined, further ad requests are blocked, existing cached ad inventory is invalidated, and active banners are immediately removed.
  • Fail-Safe Policy: If consent information cannot be verified or an error occurs during consent evaluation, the game prohibits ad loading to protect user privacy.
Android System-Level Privacy Controls: You can reset or delete your Google Advertising ID at any time through your Android device settings under Settings → Google → Ads → Reset advertising ID / Delete advertising ID. You may also enable “Opt out of Ads Personalization” on compatible Android versions.

6 Android Permissions Requested

In strict adherence to the principle of minimal privilege, Tower Drop: Lava Escape requests only two non-sensitive Android permissions:

  • android.permission.INTERNET: Required to establish encrypted HTTPS network communication with Google AdMob servers for ad loading and web asset runtime execution.
  • android.permission.VIBRATE: Required to trigger tactile haptic vibration pulses during platform impacts, smash combos, hazard alerts, and victory fanfare.
Zero Sensitive Permissions: The app does not request Camera, Microphone, Read Contacts, Read External Storage, Fine GPS Location, Read Phone State, or SMS permissions.
